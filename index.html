Quick start

From blank field to full plan

  1. 01
    Load a map

    Upload a picture of your field, open a saved map pack, or pick a preset.

  2. 02
    Check the bunkers

    Green bunkers are detected automatically. Placing bunkers by hand with Structure and Sizer gives the best result, or try the experimental Auto tool for red and blue layouts and fix what it misses.

  3. 03
    Place the teams

    Use Players to drop Team 1, then Team 2, and lock them in.

  4. 04
    Plan the plays

    Draw routes with Brush, check sight lines with Vision, and build a shoot scene.

  5. 05
    Save and share

    Save a map pack to pick up later, or download your scene as a GIF.

Map

Getting your field in

Map

Upload map image

Load a PNG or JPG of your field. Bright green bunkers on the image turn into structures automatically. For other layouts, like red and blue bunkers on white, place bunkers with Structure for the best result, or try the experimental Auto tool.

Map

Map packs

Save map pack downloads a .zip with your map and everything on it: bunkers, intel, and shoot scenes. Upload map pack opens that .zip (or the unzipped folder) to pick up where you left off.

Map

Preset map packs

Load a ready-made layout in one tap. The first preset is MID-ATLANTIC 2026, with every bunker already mapped.

Tools

Every tool, step by step

Tool

Move

Your everyday pointer for fine-tuning a setup once the teams are locked.

  • Drag any locked player to a new spot.
  • Tap a bunker to select it. Its name and notes open in the Field intel panel under the map.
  • With Design on, a selected bunker can get its own fill color in the Design panel.

TipUse Move after placing teams to stagger players behind different bunkers.

Tool

Players

Drop a whole team onto the field at once, then lock it in.

  • Choose how many players are on the team, from 1 to 10, with the + and − buttons.
  • Tap the spawn point. A ghost stack of players appears there. Tap somewhere else to move it.
  • Nudge the whole stack with the arrow pad beside it, then tap ✓ to lock the team.
  • Team 2 unlocks once Team 1 is locked. Place it the same way on the other side.
  • Reset team placement clears both teams so you can start over. On mobile, it's under Save map pack.

TipChanging the player count while the ghosts are showing re-stacks the team on the spot.

Tool

Brush

Sketch routes, lanes, and callouts freehand on top of the map.

  • Drag on the map to draw.
  • Set the brush size anywhere from 2 to 40 with the slider.
  • Tap a quick color (lime, orange, blue, yellow, pink, or white) or pick any custom color.
  • Clear drawings wipes every stroke. Bunkers, players, and scenes stay put.
  • On mobile, the size, color, and clear controls open right under the tool buttons when Brush is picked.

TipDrawings are for planning on screen and aren't saved in map packs.

Tool

Structure

Add any bunkers the map missed, or build a layout from scratch.

  • Pick a shape: Rectangle, Circle, or Triangle.
  • Pick a wall style: Filled, Border only, or Transparent wall. All three block vision and shoot lines.
  • Drag on an open spot to draw the bunker to size.
  • Drag an existing bunker to move it, or tap Undo to remove the last one you placed.

TipBright green bunkers on an uploaded image are detected for you, so you only add what's missing.

Tool

Auto

Experimental

Turn a printed field layout into structures in one pass.

Best resultsAuto is still experimental and can miss or misshape bunkers. For the most accurate field, place bunkers by hand with Structure and fine-tune them with Sizer, or use Auto as a starting point and fix what it gets wrong.

  • Drag a box on the map around the part you want to scan.
  • Brush white over anything that isn't a bunker, like text, logos, and lines, then press Done.
  • Every bunker color in the box is detected, and each bunker gets the best-fitting Rectangle, Circle, or Triangle.
  • Each color shows under Colors, and its × removes that color's bunkers. Tap a bunker to add a color it missed, or tap Edit to fix the whiteout and scan again.
  • Undo steps back one action at a time, and Undo all puts the map back how it was.
  • Odd shapes like L-walls and diagonal snakes are built from several pieces, so lanes stay blocked.
  • Black outlines, stripes, and dark shading on a bunker count as part of it, so it maps as one piece. Turn off Include black mixed in to skip them.

TipRaise Color match for faded or shaded bunkers. Maps with no green bunkers open on Auto.

Tool

Vision

See exactly what a player can see from where they stand.

  • Tap a player to cast pink sight lines all the way around them.
  • Each line stops at the first bunker it hits, so open lanes and blind spots stand out.
  • Tap the same player again to turn the lines off, or tap another player to switch.
  • Lines stay on when you change tools and follow the player during a run.

TipTurn on Vision, then play a scene to watch a runner's view open up as they move.

Tool

Shoot

Plan each player's move: where they run and what they shoot at.

  • Tap a player, then use Draw run and drag from them (or anywhere on the map) to draw the exact route they run.
  • Set the run time, from 0.5 to 30 seconds.
  • Use Set shoot to tap a spot, or turn on Make shoot point a player and tap an opponent.
  • Run selected player previews the move. The player returns to their start spot when it ends.
  • Save player setup adds them to the timeline. Then use + Add another player for the next one.
  • ‹ Previous and Next player › step through the same team in order, looping back around. A finished setup saves itself when you switch.

TipOn a phone, once a player is picked, tapping a teammate draws instead of switching, so close stacks don't steal your selection. Use Previous and Next to change players. See how scenes work.

Tool

Sizer

Fine-tune any bunker's size, position, and style.

  • Tap a bunker to select it. Its shape shows at the top of the panel.
  • Set the exact Width and Height with the sliders.
  • Drag the bunker, or nudge it with the arrow pad. The Move step slider sets how far each tap moves it.
  • Switch its wall style between Filled, Border only, and Transparent wall.
  • Duplicate copies it, Delete removes it, and Undo Sizer change reverses your last edit.

TipDrop the Move step low for pixel-perfect lineups, or raise it to slide bunkers across the field fast.

Tool

50 Mirror

Map one half of the field, then copy it to the other side.

  • Tap 50 Mirror and confirm.
  • Drag a box around the part of the field you want to mirror.
  • Click two points along the 50 line.
  • Bunkers on the side with more bunkers are copied across the line, and triangles flip to face the right way.
  • Spots that already have a bunker are skipped, so nothing gets doubled up.

TipPress Esc at any step to cancel.

Tool

Design

Swap the photo for a clean, custom-colored field drawn from your bunkers.

  • Tap Design to open its panel, and flip between photo and design with the Design map switch.
  • Structures tab: choose from 15 color combos, or set the fill, outline, and glow for every bunker.
  • Select a bunker with Move or Sizer to give it its own fill. Clear custom fills resets them.
  • Canvas tab: choose from 15 field themes, or set the turf, stripes, grid lines, boundary, and vignette.

TipMap packs and presets open in Design automatically, and your colors are saved with the pack.

Panel

Field intel

Name every bunker and keep your team's notes in one place.

  • Shows under the map with Move, Brush, and Vision. On mobile, it's the Intel button.
  • Tap a green bunker, then give it a name and notes, like "Snake 1, watch the wire".
  • Open the Full report to see every bunker's intel at once and write a report for the whole field.

TipIntel is saved in your map pack, so the whole team gets the same callouts.

Shoot & run

Build a scene and play it out

Shoot

Shoot and run

Tap a player, then drag to draw their run route any shape you want and set a run time. Add a shoot point (a spot on the map or an opposing player). The yellow shoot line turns red and stops where a bunker blocks it.

Shoot

Shoot options

Stand still shoots without running. No shoot just runs. Shoot over sends the line straight over bunkers to the target.

Shoot

Scenes

Save a setup for each player, then hit Play scene to run them all at once. Green paint dots fly down every shoot line. Tap a clip in the timeline to edit that player, or drag the end of the clip to change their run time. Players return to their start spots as soon as the scene ends, or any time with Reset scene.

Shoot

Play in window

Watch the scene big and centered in its own window. Great for walking your team through a play.

Shoot

Download GIF

Record the scene as a looping GIF you can drop in the group chat or team doc.
