Field Mapper guide

Map it.
Plan it.

Field Mapper turns a picture of your field into a planning board. Load the layout, drop both teams on it, and plan every lane, shot, and run. Here's what each tool does.

Open Field Mapper How to start

Quick start

From blank field to full plan

  1. Load a map. Upload a picture of your field, open a saved map pack, or pick a preset.
  2. Check the bunkers. Green bunkers are detected automatically. Add or fix any with Structure and Sizer.
  3. Place the teams. Use Players to drop Team 1, then Team 2, and lock them in.
  4. Plan the plays. Draw routes with Brush, check sight lines with Vision, and build a shoot scene.
  5. Save and share. Save a map pack to pick up later, or download your scene as a GIF.

Map

Getting your field in

🖼️

Upload map image

Load a PNG or JPG of your field. Bright green bunkers on the image turn into structures automatically.

📦

Map packs

Save map pack downloads a .zip with your map and everything on it: bunkers, intel, player names, and shoot scenes. Upload map pack opens that .zip (or the unzipped folder) to pick up where you left off.

⭐

Preset map packs

Load a ready-made layout in one tap. The first preset is MID-ATLANTIC 2026, with every bunker already mapped.

Tools

Everything on the tool bar

↖

Move

Drag any player to a new spot. Tap a player to give them a name, which shows over their head and follows them on runs. Leave it blank to show nothing.

⚑

Players

Place a whole team at once. Pick 1–10 players with the + and − buttons, then tap where they spawn. A ghost preview shows where they'll go. Nudge it with the arrows, then tap ✓ to lock. Do Team 1 first, then Team 2. Once locked, players can be dragged one at a time and named from the list (Team 1 - 1, Team 1 - 2, …).

🖌

Brush

Draw routes, lanes, and notes right on the map. Pick a brush size and color, and use Clear drawings to start fresh.

▦

Structure

Drag on the map to add a bunker: rectangle, circle, or triangle. Pick a wall style: Filled, Border only, or Transparent wall. All three still block vision and shoot lines. Press on an existing bunker to drag it, and use Undo to remove the last one you placed.

👁

Vision

Tap a player to see pink sight lines in every direction. Lines stop where a bunker blocks the view, so you can spot open lanes and blind spots. The lines stay on when you switch tools.

⇲

Sizer

Tap a bunker to edit it. Set its exact width and height, change its wall style, and move it by dragging or with the arrow pad. Duplicate makes an exact copy, Delete removes it, and Undo Sizer change reverses your last edit.

⇋

50 Mirror

Map one half of the field and copy it to the other side. Confirm, drag a box around the field, then click two points on the 50 line. The bunkers on the busier side are mirrored across it, and triangles flip to face the right way.

📋

Intel

Tap a green bunker to give it a name and notes, like "Snake 1, watch the wire". Fill in a field report for the whole map, and open the full intel view to see every bunker at once.

Shoot & run

Build a scene and play it out

🎯

Shoot and run

Tap a player, set a shoot point (a spot on the map or an opposing player), and a run point with a run time. The yellow shoot line turns red and stops where a bunker blocks it.

⚙️

Shot options

Stand still shoots without running. No shot just runs. Shoot over sends the line straight over bunkers to the target.

🎬

Scenes

Save a setup for each player, then hit Play scene to run them all at once. Green paint dots fly down every shoot line. Players return to their start spots 10 seconds after the scene ends, or right away with Reset scene.

🖥️

Play in window

Watch the scene big and centered in its own window. Great for walking your team through a play.

🎞️

Download GIF

Record the scene as a looping GIF you can drop in the group chat or team doc.
